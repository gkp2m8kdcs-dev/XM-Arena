Private by Design

All data lives in your iOS sandbox. No analytics SDK, no remote server, no third party. Delete the app — data is gone.

The dashboard is the home of your portfolio.

Glance at your net worth, lifetime return and day change in a single hero card. A smooth range selector lets you zoom through one week, one month, three months, six months, one year or all time — instantly.

  • Tap any point on the chart to surface a precision cursor with date and value
  • Allocation donut breaks down exposure by asset class and region
  • Two risk meters surface concentration and maximum drawdown

Holdings with full context.

Every position has its own interactive thirty-day price trend, performance grid, annualized return, dividend total and a six-month cost-versus-return bar chart. Search, filter and sort to find what you need in seconds.

  • Filter by asset type — stock, ETF, crypto, bond, cash
  • Sort by value, weight, cost or return
  • Tags and notes for personal context on every position

Four analytical views.

The insights section delivers Performance, Allocation, Cash Flow and Compare modes — plus a yield heatmap that surfaces where your income is coming from.

  • 180-day portfolio trend with Sharpe ratio and max drawdown
  • Monthly inflows and outflows alongside invested and returned capital
  • Top five holdings ranked by value and return

Designed like a private notebook.

Every interaction is built with SwiftUI and runs natively on iOS 16 and later. The draggable chart cursor, the tap-to-dismiss keyboards, the bouncing transaction rows — each detail is engineered for calm.
