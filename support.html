Quick Tips

📊

Add your first holding

Open Holdings → tap the + button. Pick the asset type, enter symbol, quantity and average cost. You can edit later.

📓

Log a transaction

Open Activity → tap +. Choose from buy, sell, dividend, split, deposit, withdrawal, fee or interest.

📷

Attach a receipt

Inside any transaction, tap the camera or photo icon. Receipts are saved privately on your device.

📤

Export your data

Open Reports → tap Export. Choose transactions, holdings or dividends. Files are saved as CSV.

🎯

Track a goal

Open Goals → tap +. Pick a template or build a custom one with your own deadline and target amount.

⚙️

Switch currency

Open Settings → Display → Currency. Choose from a list of supported currencies.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is XM Arena really free?

Yes. XM Arena is free to download with no subscriptions, no premium tiers, no in-app purchases and no advertisements. We plan to keep it that way.

Where is my data stored?

All of your data is stored locally on your device, inside the iOS app sandbox. We do not have a server, do not sync to the cloud, and do not transmit your portfolio to anyone. Uninstalling the app permanently removes all data.

Do I need an account?

No. XM Arena does not require sign-up, login, email or any form of identity verification. Install it and start journaling immediately.

Does XM Arena connect to my brokerage or bank?

No. XM Arena is a manual journal. You record all transactions, prices and dividend information yourself. The app never asks for brokerage credentials, never accesses your bank, and never makes outbound requests.

Can I export my data?

Yes. The Reports section lets you export transactions, holdings and dividends as CSV files. You can share these via AirDrop, Mail, Files, or any other iOS share extension.

Does the app provide live market quotes?

No. XM Arena is a journaling and analysis tool only. All pricing data is supplied by you. We do not provide real-time, delayed or any other form of market data feed.

What devices and iOS versions are supported?

XM Arena requires iOS 16 or later and runs on iPhone. It is built natively in SwiftUI for the best performance and visual quality.

How do I back up my data?

Use the CSV export feature inside the Reports section. Save the files to Files, iCloud Drive, or any other location of your choice. The app itself does not perform automatic cloud backup because we have no server to receive it.

I lost my data after reinstalling — can you help?

Because we never receive your data, we cannot recover it for you. This is by design — your portfolio stays private and never leaves your device. To prevent data loss, please use the CSV export feature regularly to keep an off-device backup.
