Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026 · Effective immediately

The short version

XM Arena collects nothing. There is no analytics, no account, no cloud sync, no remote server, and no third-party SDK. Every piece of data you record — transactions, holdings, notes, receipt photos — is stored locally on your device inside the app's sandbox and is removed when you delete the app.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains what information XM Arena ("the App", "we", "us") collects, uses, and discloses. We have written this policy in plain language because we believe privacy disclosures should be readable, not just legal.

By using XM Arena you agree to the terms set out in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not install or use the App.

2. Information We Collect

The simple answer: nothing leaves your device. Specifically:

Personal Information Not collected

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, mailing address, date of birth, government identifier, or any other personal information. XM Arena does not ask you to register or create an account.

Portfolio & Transaction Data Stored locally only

Any holdings, transactions, prices, notes, tags, dividend records, watchlist entries, goal information, and receipt photos you record are saved exclusively inside the iOS app sandbox on your device. This data is never transmitted to us or to any third party.

Usage Analytics Not collected

XM Arena contains no analytics SDK (no Firebase, no Mixpanel, no Amplitude, no Crashlytics, no Adjust, no AppsFlyer, no Facebook SDK, and no equivalent). We do not log taps, screens, sessions, device identifiers (IDFA/IDFV), or any behavioral telemetry.

Device & Diagnostic Data Not collected

We do not collect device identifiers, IP addresses, advertising IDs, crash reports, performance metrics, or diagnostic logs. Apple may surface anonymous, aggregated App Store information to us as the developer, but this contains no data originating from inside XM Arena.

3. Permissions We Request

XM Arena only asks for the system permissions it strictly needs to function. Each prompt is shown with a plain-language reason in line with Apple's Human Interface Guidelines.

  • Camera — used solely to attach receipt photos to a transaction you are creating. Photos are never uploaded or shared.
  • Photo Library (read) — used solely to pick an existing image to attach to a transaction.
  • Photo Library (write) — used solely when you explicitly export a chart or report as an image.

You can revoke any of these permissions at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security. The App will continue to work, with the affected feature disabled.

4. How We Use Information

Because we do not collect information, we do not use it for any purpose — including advertising, marketing, profiling, retargeting, or sale. The only "processing" that happens to your data is the on-device calculation of portfolio metrics (net worth, returns, dividends, allocation) performed entirely inside the App.

5. Sharing of Information

XM Arena does not share information with anyone because we do not have any information to share. There are no advertising networks, no analytics providers, no data brokers, no marketing partners, and no service providers that receive your data.

6. Data Storage and Retention

All App data is stored locally on your iPhone or iPad within the standard iOS app sandbox. We do not retain any server-side copy. When you uninstall XM Arena, the system removes the sandbox container and all data inside it. We do not have the ability to recover that data.

If you wish to back up your data, you can export it as CSV files from the Reports section of the App at any time.

7. Children's Privacy

XM Arena is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because we collect no information at all from any user, this principle is enforced by design.

8. International Transfers

Since no data leaves your device, there are no international transfers to disclose. Your data does not cross borders because it never crosses our servers — they do not exist.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (for example the EEA under GDPR, the United Kingdom under the UK GDPR, California under the CCPA/CPRA, or Brazil under the LGPD), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, port, or restrict the processing of your personal data.

Because all of your data lives on your device and can be reviewed, edited, exported, or deleted by you at any time through the App's interface, these rights are satisfied by design. You may also uninstall the App to remove all data instantly.

10. Security

Your data benefits from the security features of the iOS platform, including full-disk encryption, app sandbox isolation, and per-permission access controls. Because the App does not transmit data over the network, your portfolio is not exposed to network interception, credential theft, or server-side breaches.

You can further protect your data by enabling a device passcode, Face ID, or Touch ID.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make a material change, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, provide an in-app notice. Because we collect no data, no change to this policy can retroactively affect information we never held.

13. Effective Date
